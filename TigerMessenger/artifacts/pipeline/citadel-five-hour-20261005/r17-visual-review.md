# r17 independent visual review — reject candidate

Actually viewed r16/r17 `left-terrace-day-noCloud-plants.png` and `bay-ridge-day-noCloud-plants.png`, four real screenshots. r17's central-right cliff has stronger, closely packed vertical folds descending from the summit and new thin horizontal green strips. The wider bay view still shows this hanging-sheet silhouette. r16 has unresolved paper facets, but r17 is not a clear visual improvement. Do not award improvement for reduced vertical multihits. Recommendation: keep `citadelBaySurfacePass=0` for this batch.

Additional offline reconstruction using the real r15 castle matrix confirms the defect already exists before landform. `r17-bay-aspect-diagnosis.json` measures longest edge / smallest altitude: the 768 selected children have median 16.97 and P95 164.89; 123 transition fan triangles have median 22.41, P95 208.02, maximum 567. The worst transition spans 26.18 m with 23.06 m height difference. Thus the nonlinear bay radial cut still stretches extremely small original edges into long strips, and the conforming fan worsens some shapes. Downstream landform could additionally compress them, but without r17 per-face trace it cannot be assigned a measured share of the visible defect.

No further production changes made. Another uniform increase of n does not address parameter-space anisotropy or the fan's long centroid connections. A future attempt would require an explicit bay seam surface construction or metric-adaptive triangulation with quality guards, plus full protected-boundary checks. That is a larger, separately reviewed change, not a justified last-minute patch.
