# Recorded firearm sources
The Free Firearm Sound Library — Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney. CC0 1.0.
Collection and original license statement: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Selective mirror: https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-asset-kit/tree/main/sfx/firearm_sfx/Prepared%20SFX%20Library
- Locust: AK-47/C_27P.wav, two recorded takes.
- Beetle: Carl Gustav M45/G_20P.wav, three recorded takes.
- Ant: Nova/O_17P.wav, two recorded takes, pitched down at runtime to support the fictional pressure cannon.
Transform: trim silence, mix stereo to mono, remove DC, resample 44.1kHz/16-bit, peak -1.1dBFS, fade final 120ms. Exact offsets and original hashes in manifest.json. Rebuild: tools/audio/prepare_robot_recordings.py <original-directory>.
No Call of Duty recordings or extracted game assets. The requested audio character is a design reference, not an assertion of identical sounds or equivalent audio quality.
